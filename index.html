Gliphy Privacy Policy

Gliphy (the Glyph keyboard) · Keypoint Technologies (UK) Ltd · Effective 25 November 2026

In short

  • Typing, autocorrect, suggestions and Glide run on your iPhone. The text you type is not sent anywhere unless you turn on Typing Research.
  • In beta (TestFlight) builds, Typing Research is on by default, as beta testers are told when they join. Recordings are encrypted on your iPhone before they upload automatically, and only we can read them. You can turn it off at any time.
  • GIF and sticker searches send only the words you type into that search box to our media provider, KLIPY.
  • No ads, no tracking, no analytics SDKs, and we never sell data.

Who we are

Gliphy is a keyboard for iPhone made by Keypoint Technologies (UK) Ltd ("we", "us"). This policy covers the Gliphy app and its keyboard extension. During testing, the keyboard appears on your iPhone as "Glyph".

What stays on your iPhone

To give you suggestions and corrections, the keyboard processes what you type on your device. It keeps the following in the app's private storage on your iPhone:

  • Personalization: word and context counts that improve suggestions, plus how your Glide strokes are adapted.
  • Emoji recents and clipboard history (clipboard history needs Full Access).
  • Appearance: your theme and color choices.
  • Diagnostics: aggregate counters such as latency, memory use and storage health. They never contain the text you type.

None of this leaves your iPhone unless you join Typing Research (see below). You can remove it at any time in the app with Reset learned words or Delete all keyboard data.

Fields we never touch

The keyboard does not learn from or record password and other secure fields. Typing Research also never records email, phone, number, address, name, username and one-time-code fields, or anything you type with another keyboard.

Full Access

The keyboard works without Full Access. If you allow it, Full Access enables settings shared between the app and keyboard, saved on-device learning, clipboard history, and GIF and sticker search. You can turn Full Access off at any time in iOS Settings › General › Keyboard › Keyboards › Glyph.

GIF and sticker search (KLIPY)

GIFs and stickers come from KLIPY. When you search, the keyboard sends only the search words you typed into the GIF or sticker search box, over an encrypted HTTPS connection. The rest of what you type is never included. Like any web request, KLIPY also receives your device's IP address. Their handling of that data is covered by KLIPY's privacy policy, available at klipy.com.

Typing Research (optional, beta builds only)

Typing Research helps us improve autocorrect and Glide. In beta (TestFlight) builds it is on by default; beta testers are told this when they join the beta. You can turn it off at any time in the app under Privacy › Typing Research.

What is recorded while it is on:

  • The words you type in ordinary text fields, and the suggestions, corrections and undos around them.
  • Where and when your finger touched the keys, your Glide (swipe) paths, and the keyboard's layout.
  • Device model, iOS version, language setting and app version.
  • A random install ID created when you join, so we can tell recordings apart.
  • If you choose to fill them in: a nickname or tester code, your typing hand, and how you usually type.
  • The aggregate diagnostics counters described above.

How it is protected: recordings are stored with iOS's strongest file protection, so they cannot be read while your iPhone is locked. Before upload, they are encrypted on your iPhone with a key only we hold. They are then stored in Apple's iCloud (CloudKit) service, which requires you to be signed in to iCloud. Anyone else who obtains the stored files sees only encrypted data, plus the install ID, file size and app build number.

How it is used: only to improve Gliphy's typing, autocorrect and Glide. It is never sold, shared for advertising, or used to identify you.

Ordinary messages can contain personal things. Turn research off before typing anything you would not want read.

When it uploads: automatically, when you open or leave the app and every few hours in the background. There is no need to upload anything yourself.

Stopping and deleting: turn the switch off at any time. Stop and delete my recordings on this iPhone also removes anything not yet uploaded. To have uploaded recordings deleted, email us with your nickname or tester code and we will delete them.

Retention

On your iPhone, research recordings waiting to upload are kept for at most 30 days, or until they reach 200 MB, whichever comes first. Uploaded research recordings are kept only as long as we need them to improve the keyboard, and are deleted when you ask. Everything else stays on your device until you delete it or remove the app.

What we don't do

  • No advertising, and no ad or analytics SDKs.
  • No tracking across apps or websites.
  • No selling or renting of data.
  • No accounts: Gliphy does not ask you to sign up.

Children

Gliphy is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them through Typing Research. If you believe a child has joined Typing Research, contact us and we will delete their recordings.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, including the UK and EU, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your data, or object to how it is used. Most of your data never leaves your iPhone and is under your control in the app. For uploaded research recordings, email us and we will respond within 30 days. You can also complain to your local data protection authority; in the UK, that is the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page and its effective date. Significant changes will also be shown in the app.

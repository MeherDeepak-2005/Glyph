# Generated NSS unigram dictionary

187,462 word forms; 73,174 additions; no removals.

38,548 additions have measured occurrences in the human-source inputs; 34,626 have no such observations and use explicit smoothing.

All additions are in [ADDED_WORDS.txt](ADDED_WORDS.txt). Per-word source counts and weights are in [ADDED_WORDS.tsv](ADDED_WORDS.tsv); the complete model ledger is [unigrams.tsv](unigrams.tsv).

Pack size: 9,254,997 bytes. Zero bigrams, trigrams, starter rows and phrase contexts. The CIFG model and its output vocabulary are unchanged.

Frequency estimates are calibrated on validation, not invented word counts. SODA synthetic dialogue counts are excluded from the frequency model. See manifest.json for exact source hashes, mixture trials and limitations.
